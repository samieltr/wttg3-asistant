The Johnson family is locked away in a remote location and filmed via one wide lens camera. Previous day footage is shared the following day. No livestream will ever be available.

The family is aware of their situation and can see a timer that updates three times per day with the minutes they have left in Shelter. The timer can never exceed 9999 minutes.

Periodically the family will receive a challenge that if completed decreases their time left in Shelter. Viewers who increase the remaining time encourage family members to accept the challenge terms. Consider subscribing or donating for more thrilling content.
